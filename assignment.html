# Component Diagram (SVG) Precise function / primary uses
1 Resistor Opposes current flow by converting electrical energy into heat. Used to limit current, divide voltage, and set bias points.
2 Capacitor (non‑polar) Stores energy in an electric field between two plates. Blocks DC, passes AC; used for coupling, filtering, and timing.
3 Capacitor (polarized) Stores energy in an electric field with a fixed polarity (electrolytic). Used for large‑value smoothing and bulk energy storage in power supplies.
4 Inductor Stores energy in a magnetic field when current flows. Opposes changes in current; used in filters, chokes, and transformers.
5 Variable resistor (pot) Adjustable resistance via a moving contact (wiper). Used for volume control, calibration, and user‑adjustable settings.
6 Variable capacitor Adjustable capacitance (meshed plates). Changes resonant frequency in radio tuning and impedance matching.
7 Variable inductor Adjustable inductance (sliding core). Fine‑tunes RF circuits, oscillators, and filters.
8 Transformer Transfers energy between two coils via magnetic field. Steps voltage up/down and provides galvanic isolation.
9 Fuse Melts to break the circuit when current exceeds a safe limit. One‑time overcurrent protection.
10 Circuit breaker Resettable overcurrent protection. Opens a switch (thermal or magnetic) and can be manually reset.
11 Crystal oscillator Piezo‑electric resonance generates a highly stable clock frequency. Used in microcontrollers, radios, and timers.
12 Ceramic resonator Mechanical resonance (lower cost, less precise than crystal). Clock source for consumer electronics.
13 Thermistor Resistance changes with temperature. Used for temperature sensing, inrush limiting, and thermal protection.
14 Varistor (MOV) Voltage‑dependent resistor. Clamps voltage spikes (surge protection) by becoming conductive above a threshold.
15 Photoresistor (LDR) Resistance decreases with light intensity. Used in light‑sensing circuits, automatic lighting, and alarms.
16 Trimmer potentiometer Small adjustable resistor for fine calibration. Set once and left alone (bias, offset, tuning).
17 Choke Inductor optimized to block high‑frequency AC while passing DC. Used for EMI suppression and power filtering.
18 Ferrite bead Dissipates high‑frequency noise as heat. Placed in series to suppress EMI on power or signal lines.
19 Antenna Converts electrical signals into radio waves (and vice versa). Used for wireless transmission and reception.
20 Piezoelectric buzzer Converts electrical oscillations into sound via a piezo element. Used for alarms, tones, and beeps.
21 Diode Allows current in one direction only. Used for rectification, clamping, and protecting against reverse polarity.
22 LED Emits light when forward‑biased. Used as indicators, displays, and in optoelectronics.
23 Zener diode Conducts in reverse when a specific voltage is reached (Zener voltage). Used for voltage regulation and reference.
24 Schottky diode Very fast switching, low forward voltage drop. Used in high‑frequency rectifiers and solar applications.
25 Photodiode Generates current when exposed to light. Used in light sensors, optocouplers, and remote receivers.
26 NPN transistor Small base current controls large collector current. Used for amplification and switching.
27 PNP transistor Small base current controls large collector current (opposite polarity to NPN). Used in complementary switching circuits.
28 N‑channel MOSFET Voltage at gate controls current flow. High‑efficiency power switching, motor control, and PWM.
29 P‑channel MOSFET Voltage at gate controls current flow (opposite polarity to N‑channel). Used for load switching and reverse‑polarity protection.
30 JFET Voltage at gate controls current (high input impedance). Used for low‑noise amplification and analog switches.
31 SCR (Thyristor) Latches ON once triggered and stays on until current stops. Used for power control and latching switches.
32 Triac Bidirectional latching switch for AC. Used in dimmers, motor speed controls, and AC power switching.
33 Diac Bidirectional trigger diode. Conducts only above a breakover voltage; used to trigger triacs.
34 UJT Triggers when emitter voltage reaches a peak. Used in relaxation oscillators and timing circuits.
35 Phototransistor Light controls transistor current. Used in optocouplers, light sensors, and punch‑through detectors.
36 Optocoupler Transfers signal using light, with no electrical connection. Provides galvanic isolation between circuits.
37 Voltage regulator (78xx) Maintains a fixed output voltage regardless of input or load changes. Used for stable power supplies.
38 LM317 (adjustable) Maintains an adjustable output voltage set by external resistors. Used in lab power supplies and custom rails.
39 Op‑amp (741) Amplifies the difference between two inputs. Used for amplification, filtering, comparison, and mathematical operations.
40 Comparator (LM339) Outputs high or low based on which input is larger. Used for level detection, zero‑crossing, and threshold alarms.
41 Timer IC (555) Generates precise timing pulses or oscillations. Used for timers, PWM, and pulse generation.
42 Microcontroller Executes programmed instructions. Used for embedded control, automation, and IoT devices.
43 EEPROM Non‑volatile memory that can be erased electrically. Stores configuration and small data that must survive power‑off.
44 SRAM Fast volatile memory that holds data as long as power is on. Used for CPU cache and temporary buffers.
45 DRAM Volatile memory that must be refreshed constantly. Used for main memory in computers (high density, low cost).
46 Flash memory Non‑volatile memory erased in blocks. Used for firmware, USB drives, and SSDs.
47 Shift register Converts serial data to parallel (or vice versa). Expands I/O pins, drives LED matrices.
48 Multiplexer Selects one of many inputs and routes it to a single output. Used for data selection and signal routing.
49 Demultiplexer Routes a single input to one of many outputs. Used for data distribution and memory addressing.
50 Logic gate (AND) Output is HIGH only when all inputs are HIGH. Fundamental building block of digital logic.
